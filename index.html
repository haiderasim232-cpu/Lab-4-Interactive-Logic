The beginning

The mountain is waiting.

You arrive at base camp with three energy points. A trail leads toward a fork in the mountain. Will you begin the climb?

Walking uses energy. Resting restores one energy point, up to a maximum of three.

How to play

Read the story and choose your next action. Watch your energy: the steep route requires more energy than the forest path. You can rest before continuing your journey.

Find the summit, discover the lake, or return to camp. Restart to try a different route.
